OUR SERVICES

Our Services

Aluminium Fences

Powder-coated fences in custom heights and colours, built to last with almost no maintenance.

Aluminium Pergolas

Pergolas with adjustable louvred roofs, optional motors and LED lighting, made to measure.

Aluminium Railings

Glass and aluminium railings for balconies, staircases and terraces, safe and corrosion-resistant.

Decking

Slip-resistant, UV-resistant composite and aluminium decking for pools and terraces.

Iron Railings

Ornamental wrought iron railings, hot-dip galvanised, custom made or restored.

Polycarbonate Sheets

Solid and multi-wall roofing sheets with UV protection, cut to size for your project.

Photovoltaic Systems

Roof and ground mounting structures and complete solar installations, on-grid or off-grid.

Stainless Steel

Marine-grade balustrades, handrails and custom pieces in a polished or brushed finish.

Decades of Excellence In Manufacturing

With over 60 years of experience in the aluminium and metal fabrication industry, Dracoudis has established itself as a leader in providing high-quality solutions for both residential and commercial projects.
